01

Automated TikToks

Faceless story videos are a script, a synthetic voice, word-by-word captions, and background footage. Doing that by hand means writing, recording, timing every word, cutting, then guessing why people left.

This program runs that whole job on my Mac. Scroll through the phone: one example video is picked, written, spoken, captioned, cut, and then the drop-off is turned into a rule for the next one. Text, voice, alignment, and the cut all stay on the machine.

FaceLook Hero Mode

FaceLook is a school site for learning names and faces. Hero Mode is a long run of questions. A wrong answer or a slow one costs health, so a high score means hundreds of correct clicks without a miss.

Scroll the phone. A name round and a face round play out, then the script reacts, skips the pause, and waits until the photos match the name.

Railway

Deine Strecke

Scroll and the train moves from the coal mine, past iron, out to gold. Each mine pays the next piece of track. The train unloads at the station, and that stock is the only money in the game.

github.com/cortenburg28-arch/deine-strecke

The yard

What the ore buys

A longer train is worth it because one trip carries more. The gold train holds five loads. The first steam locomotive holds one, so the same mine takes five trips. Bars below are loads per trip, not speed.

At the station the train waits while it unloads. A goods shed, paid in steel, cuts that wait from 3.5 seconds to 2. A fast ramp, paid in steel and a gold bar, cuts it to 1 second. Steel and gold bars come from the smelter: 2 coal + 1 iron ore, or 2 coal + 1 gold ore.
